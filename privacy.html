Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Cleo's Homelab Mail Assistant ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner, on their own Google account. It is not available to anyone else.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app uses the Gmail API to read message content and metadata, and to manage labels, filters and trash in the owner's own mailbox.

How the data is used

Mail data is used only to organise the owner's inbox. It is processed on the owner's own server. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on that server.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

No copy of the mailbox is kept beyond what the task in progress needs. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections, which immediately stops the app from reaching the mailbox.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email listed on this app's Google consent screen.
